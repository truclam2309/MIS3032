## Retrieval rules

- Requirement and Business Rule have priority over product interpretation or prototype behavior.
- User Research supports evidence about needs and pain points; it does not override confirmed Requirements.
- Product, Design, Technical and Testing documents must reference the relevant Requirement or Business Rule.
- If information is not confirmed in the Vault, record it as an Open Question instead of inventing a decision.
